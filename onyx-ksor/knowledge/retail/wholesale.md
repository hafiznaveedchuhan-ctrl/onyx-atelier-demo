We supply wine and liquor stores and high-end gift boutiques across Albany,
Saratoga Springs and the Capital Region.

## How it works

1. **Request the line sheet** on the For Retailers page: wholesale prices,
   minimum order and lead times.
2. **See samples in your store.** The owner brings pieces so buyers can see the
   stone in person.
3. **Place a first order.** The owner helps plan the shelf display.

Wholesale prices and minimums come from the owner only.
