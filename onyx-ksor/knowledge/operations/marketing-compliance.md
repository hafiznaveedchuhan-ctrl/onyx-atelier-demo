## Ads and posts

- Meta treats alcohol accessories as non-alcohol products when the ad does not
  depict alcohol or its consumption. Default creative: product-only shots.
- If a creative shows alcohol, run it as an alcohol ad with US 21+ targeting,
  and only with the owner's approval.
- Target area: Albany and Saratoga Springs. Audience: women 30+ and gift buyers.

## Retailer outreach email

- Real sender name, honest subject line, the business's physical address, and
  an opt-out honored within 10 business days (CAN-SPAM basics).
- Nothing is sent without the owner's approval.

## Google Business Profile

- Only eligible if the business meets customers in person. Confirm before setup.
