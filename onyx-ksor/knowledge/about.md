Onyx Atelier is the demo name for a business that sells drinkware cut from
natural onyx stone. The real brand name replaces it once the owner confirms it.

## What we sell

Four product lines: onyx wine glasses, an onyx wine chiller, an onyx shot glass
set with a tray, and an onyx beer mug. We sell drinkware only. We do not sell
wine, liquor or any other beverage.

## Where we serve

The Capital Region of New York, with a focus on Albany and Saratoga Springs.
We sell to consumers, and to local wine and liquor stores and high-end gift
boutiques.

## What makes each piece different

Every piece is natural stone, so its color and banding are unique. Two pieces
of the same product never look identical, and that is expected, not a defect.
