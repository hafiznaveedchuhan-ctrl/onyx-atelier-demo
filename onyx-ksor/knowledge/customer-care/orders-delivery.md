## How to order

Customers contact the owner directly (or ask the product assistant to pass
the question on). The owner replies with prices and delivery options.

## Local delivery

Local delivery and pickup are offered across Albany, Saratoga Springs and the
Capital Region. The owner confirms timing and cost for each order.

## What the assistant must not do

The assistant never invents prices, stock levels, delivery dates, shipping costs
or return terms. It offers to pass those questions to the owner.
