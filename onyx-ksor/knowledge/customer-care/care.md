Onyx is natural stone, so gentle care keeps it beautiful (sample guidance;
the owner confirms before launch).

## Daily care

- Hand wash with mild soap and warm water.
- Dry right away with a soft cloth.
- Do not use a dishwasher.

## Avoid

- Leaving citrus or other acidic drinks in the piece for long periods. Rinse after use.
- Sudden temperature shocks, such as moving a piece from the freezer to hot water.
- The microwave.
