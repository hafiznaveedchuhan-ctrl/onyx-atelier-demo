Onyx forms in slow layers, which is where its bands of honey, cream and green
come from. Thin walls let light through, so a glass held to a window glows.

## How it behaves

- It is heavier than glass and cool to the touch.
- It holds its temperature, which is why a chilled onyx piece keeps drinks cool.
- It is a natural stone that can react to acids, so it needs gentle care.
  See [care](../customer-care/care.md).
