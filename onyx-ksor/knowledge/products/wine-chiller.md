A solid onyx sleeve that keeps an already chilled bottle cool at the table,
without a bucket of ice water.

## How to use it

1. Place the empty sleeve in the refrigerator for about an hour (sample guidance).
2. Slide in a bottle that is already chilled.
3. Set it on the table. There is no dripping and no ice bucket.

- Heavy, stable base.
