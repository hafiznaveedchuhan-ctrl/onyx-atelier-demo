# Spec 000 addendum — UI v2 (American premium shopping site)

## Why
Client feedback: the UI must feel like a stylish, professional US shopping site.

## Design direction
Premium US home-goods retail (department-store gift floor), built on real onyx photography.
- Fonts: Bodoni Moda (display, elegant contrast) + Jost (clean geometric body).
- Palette: Stone black #1C1B19, Paper #F7F5F0, Honey #B8862F, Onyx green #46695A, Line #E4DFD5.
- Signature: real banded-onyx photography, and product renders filled with that real stone texture.

## Page anatomy (home)
announcement bar > sticky header (nav left, logo center, search / account / gift list right)
> full-bleed onyx hero > trust strip (4 promises) > "Shop by piece" product cards
> "Why onyx" editorial split > holiday gifting banner (gift-box photo)
> "Serving the Capital Region" (Saratoga photo) > retailer band > newsletter > FAQ > footer.

## Shopping behaviour (demo)
- No invented prices: cards show "Price on request".
- "Add to gift list" adds items to a drawer (cart pattern); "Request pricing" shows a confirmation (no data stored).
- Product page: gallery (render + stone close-up), badges, quantity, accordions (details, care, delivery), "Pairs well with".

## Image rules
- Only free-license images (Wikimedia Commons CC BY / CC BY-SA / PD), credited on /credits.
- No other seller's product photos. Renders are labeled illustrations until client photos arrive.
- No alcohol being poured or consumed.

## Acceptance
- Looks premium at 1366px and 390px; keyboard focus visible; reduced motion respected.
- Gift list works across pages; chat still works; build passes.

## Revision v3 (owner-supplied images)
- Owner supplied images replace all Wikimedia images. Excluded: branded products/labels (VINOVA, Cloudy Bay),
  likely retailer/stock-agency photos, and off-brand props (banana scenes).
- Every product photo is labeled "Sample photo"; /credits explains photos are samples, not the client's onyx pieces.
- Each product has a full page: gallery, tagline, specs table, story, 4 highlights, how-to, gifting, care, 4 FAQs, pairs-well-with.
